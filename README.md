# Research Overview
My doctoral research focuses on artificial intelligence, renewable energy systems, and sustainable desalination technologies, with particular emphasis on machine-learning-based modelling and multi-criteria decision support for Concentrated Solar Power (CSP)-driven Multi-Effect Distillation with Thermal Vapour Compression (MED-TVC).

# Repository Structure
bibliography/ – Scientific references managed in Zotero and exported in BibTeX format.
data-management/ – Two-page Data Management Plan covering research data, FAIR principles, storage, confidentiality, and preservation.

# Data Availability and Confidentiality
Research datasets are stored on Google Drive and backed up on a USB flash drive.
The underlying simulation data and restricted research code are not publicly shared, following supervisory guidance and applicable research collaboration arrangements.
The public repository contains documentation and bibliographic information only.

# Reproducibility and Open Science
The research adopts reproducibility and FAIR data-management principles through structured documentation, reference management, version control, and transparent methodological reporting, subject to data-access restrictions.
