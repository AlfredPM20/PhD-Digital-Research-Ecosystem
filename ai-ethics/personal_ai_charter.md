# Personal Charter for Responsible Use of Generative AI in Doctoral Research

**Alfred Pitia Martin Loro — Module M5, November 2026**

I use generative artificial intelligence as a support tool for learning, literature discovery, coding assistance, language improvement and the organisation of research ideas, not as a substitute for scientific judgement. I remain responsible for the originality, accuracy and integrity of every document, result and conclusion I submit.

**Verification and traceability.** I verify references against DOI resolvers, publisher websites and trusted bibliographic databases. I check that a paper genuinely supports the claim attributed to it. AI-generated equations, code and numerical results must be tested independently, and methods should record datasets, software versions, random seeds and validation procedures whenever applicable.

**Transparency.** When required by a course, journal or funder, I disclose the AI system, version, date and specific tasks assisted. I retain important prompts and checks, and I never list an AI tool as a scientific author.

**Confidentiality and intellectual property.** I do not upload unpublished datasets, proprietary EES models, restricted research code, peer-review manuscripts or personal data to external AI services without permission. My supervisors advise against publishing the collaborative MED–TVC research data; I respect that restriction and relevant institutional agreements.

**Responsible practice.** I review potential bias and unsupported statements, avoid invented citations, and use computational resources proportionately. AI may help me work efficiently, but methodological decisions and interpretation remain my responsibility.
